# Fold Keys

An iPhone-style keyboard for Android foldables.

- Rounded keys, letter bubble on press, lowercase labels that switch to capitals with shift
- Double-tap shift for caps lock, auto-capitals at the start of sentences
- Double-tap space for ". "
- Hold space and slide to move the cursor (trackpad mode)
- Hold delete to repeat; after a moment it deletes whole words
- Splits into two thumb halves on the inner screen (or when half-folded).
  Long-press **123** / **ABC** to split or join manually.
- Hold a letter for accents (é, ñ, ü…), slide to pick
- Emoji panel: tap the smiley key, swipe sideways, category tabs along the bottom, recents saved
- Autocorrect using the phone's spell checker, with a QuickType-style bar. Delete right after a
  correction to undo it; that word won't be corrected again
- Light and dark themes follow the system

Word list for completions: top 30,000 English words from [wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) (data CC BY-SA 4.0).

## Install

Every push builds an APK. Open the repo's **Releases** page on your phone, download
`FoldKeys.apk` from the newest "Fold Keys build", and open it. Android will ask you to allow
installs from your browser the first time. Then open the Fold Keys app and follow the two buttons.
